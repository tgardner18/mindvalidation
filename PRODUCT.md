# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults in MA, NH, and VT actively seeking a therapist or yoga instructor. They arrive emotionally vulnerable, evaluating whether Kirby is the right fit before making first contact. They are capable, self-aware people who want tools for change — not patients looking to be rescued. Context: private browsing, likely on mobile, often researching multiple providers simultaneously.

## Product Purpose

Single landing page for Mind Validation, the private therapy and yoga practice of Kirby Gardner, LCMHC. Primary goal: convert a site visit into an intake email. Success looks like a prospective client feeling confident, safe, and individually seen — enough to send that first message.

## Brand Personality

Modern · Bold · Empowering. Confident and energetic. Positions the client as capable of change, not fragile. Kirby's voice is direct, warm, and credentialed — not clinical-cold, not softly spiritual.

## Anti-references

- **Generic healthcare / insurance portal**: cold blues, clip-art stock photos, dense legal copy, institutional feel
- **Corporate therapy chain**: franchise-polished but impersonal, no individual identity, could be any provider anywhere

## Design Principles

1. **Courage over comfort**: The design should feel bold enough to signal that real change is possible — not safe/beige enough to disappear into the therapy-site crowd.
2. **Individual over institution**: Every choice should feel like Kirby, not a practice. Personal photography, direct copy, no visual patterns that could belong to a chain.
3. **Evidence earns trust**: Credentials, certifications, and named therapeutic approaches are assets, not formalities. Surface them with confidence, not apology.
4. **Empowerment over sympathy**: Copy and design speak to clients as capable people seeking tools — not as patients in need of rescue.
5. **Motion is grounding**: Animations should feel intentional and calming, never playful, bouncy, or distracting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Sufficient contrast on all text. Keyboard-navigable modal and FAQ. Reduced-motion alternatives for AOS scroll animations. No color as the sole means of conveying information.
